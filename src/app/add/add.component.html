Contribute

Add a source or a dataset

The catalog is curated by hand, by editors, against the published methodology. There is no submission form yet — that is on the roadmap, but for now the most reliable way to propose an addition is by email.

Write to index@openhistorymap.org with:

  • · A short description of the source or dataset and why it belongs in the index.
  • · A link to the canonical version (publisher, repository, archive, or PDF).
  • · The period and territory it covers, however roughly.
  • · The authors or compilers, if known.

We’ll write back. Additions are reviewed against the methodology and the index grows slowly, in public. Thank you for filling in the blanks.
